## Finding 10: MSTR vs BTC — Long-Term Performance Analysis (New)

### Historical Reality

Over 5.8 years (Aug 2020 → Jun 2026):

| Metric | BTC | MSTR |
|--------|-----|------|
| Return | +412.9% | **+874.3%** |
| CAGR | **32.4%** | **47.9%** |
| MSTR vs BTC | — | **1.48x** |

MSTR beat BTC by 1.48x. But **not** because of BTC/share growth.

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| BTC per share growth | **-30.1%** (0.002215 → 0.001548) |
| Share dilution | **+3,533%** (9.7M → 351.9M shares) |
| NAV premium expansion | **0.47x → 1.28x** (the ONLY source of outperformance) |

**The historical outperformance was entirely from NAV premium expansion, not from BTC/share compounding.** The 3,533% dilution from ATM programs destroyed BTC/share. MSTR beat BTC purely because the market started pricing MSTR at a premium to its BTC holdings — a multiple that could just as easily contract.

### Three Forward Scenarios (10-year projection, 25% BTC CAGR)

| Scenario | Final Multiple | CAGR | vs BTC |
|----------|---------------|------|--------|
| A: Hold BTC | 9.31x | **25.0%** | 1.00x |
| B: MSTR one-way | 42.96x | **45.6%** | **4.61x** |
| C: MSTR two-way | 63.97x | **51.6%** | **6.87x** |

**Scenario B (one-way) outperforms BTC primarily from assumed BTC/share growth of 15%/year.** If BTC/share growth is lower (closer to historical -6% CAGR), MSTR would track or underperform BTC.

**Scenario C (two-way) destroys BTC** because each crash-sell-recovery-buy cycle multiplies BTC/share. But this requires MSTR to actually execute the two-way strategy — selling BTC during crashes and buying back during recoveries.

### The Critical Variable: NAV Premium

MSTR's performance vs BTC is almost entirely a function of NAV premium:

| Premium Regime | What Happens | MSTR vs BTC |
|---------------|--------------|-------------|
| >2.0x | Converts highly accretive, massive BTC buying | **MSTR crushes BTC** |
| 1.0x - 2.0x | Converts work but less accretive | **MSTR tracks BTC +/-** |
| <1.0x | No accretive converts; dilution dominates | **BTC crushes MSTR** |

**Pre-2024 (0.47x NAV):** MSTR was a terrible way to own BTC. BTC itself returned 61.3% CAGR; MSTR returned 79.7% CAGR only because the premium was expanding from 0.05x.

**Post-2024 (4.0x → 1.28x NAV):** As the premium contracted from 4.0x to 1.28x, MSTR returned -33.4% CAGR while BTC returned -7.9% CAGR. The premium collapse destroyed MSTR holders.

### The Two-Way Strategy Thesis

If the two-way strategy is real, MSTR can decouple from the NAV premium trap. The BTC/share compounding from crash sells + recovery buys creates a second source of value creation independent of the premium.

Sensitivity analysis shows the two-way strategy's outperformance is robust across BTC CAGR assumptions:

| BTC CAGR | MSTR 2-way outperformance (10yr) |
|----------|----------------------------------|
| 10% | **2.02x** |
| 15% | **1.96x** |
| 25% | **1.86x** |
| 35% | **1.78x** |
| 50% | **1.68x** |

The two-way strategy works better in lower-return environments because the BTC/share compounding becomes the dominant return driver when BTC appreciation is lower.

### Bottom Line

**If MSTR stays one-way:** MSTR will track or slightly underperform BTC over full cycles, because dilution eats BTC/share gains and NAV premium is mean-reverting. The only reason to own MSTR over BTC is if you believe the NAV premium will stay elevated.

**If MSTR executes the two-way strategy:** MSTR will crush BTC by 2-7x over full cycles, because each crash-sell enables exponentially larger recovery-buys, compounding BTC/share independent of the premium.

**The single data point to watch:** STRK preferred share growth. If STRK grows as a % of MSTR's capital structure, MSTR is building the sell infrastructure for the two-way strategy. If STRK stays fixed or shrinks, MSTR is staying one-way and BTC is the better hold.
